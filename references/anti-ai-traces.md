# Anti-AI traces

Visual polish without the profession's grammar still looks synthetic. These rules apply to every domain. The profile adds the rest.

## Words

- No emoji unless the user explicitly asked and the domain is one that uses them. None of the eight profiles do.
- No lorem, TODO, TBD, FIXME, XXX, `[insert]`, 待填写, 待补充, 此处插入.
- No heading that could be moved onto another document unchanged: Overview, Summary, Key Takeaways, Key Insights, Conclusion, 概述, 总结, 关键要点, 核心洞察, 谢谢. The gate rejects these as exact slide titles.
- No filler cadence: leverage, delve, landscape (as a metaphor), robust, holistic, 赋能, 抓手, 闭环, 对齐颗粒度.
- No "as an AI".
- Prefer a short sentence, a table, or a chart over a pile of bullets. If you need a list, keep it short and parallel.

## Picture

- Do not use a color bar, an accent stripe, a sidebar, or a title underline as the motif. A source line is text, not a bar.
- Do not default to cream, beige, or a corporate blue gradient.
- Do not repeat one layout on every page. The primitives exist so the sequence can change.
- One accent, used rarely. If the colors would also "work" on an unrelated topic, they are too generic. Read the profile's dominance line before you change a token.
- Do not ship a full-bleed PNG as a slide, a chart, or a table. Native objects only.

## Facts

- Do not invent a precise statistic, a market share, a case name, a docket number, a DOI, or a 文号.
- If a number is a sketch, set `illustrative: true`. The engine prints 示意 or Illustrative.
- If the user supplied a source, name it in the source line or the assumption note.
- Quote only words you can stand behind. A paraphrase labeled as a paraphrase is better than a fake quotation.

## Finish

Read `references/visual-qa.md` against the rendered pages. The gate does not see a cramped title or a chart legend sitting on the axis.
