# Marketing / brand

Audience: a client reviewing an argument about a product or a line. Domain id is `brand` (`marketing` is an alias). More color is allowed than in consulting, and it is still typography first.

Tokens: `profiles/brand.json`. Ink `#1A120B`, paper white (not cream, not beige), one red `#9E2B25`. The red is a signal: one series, the square on a dark cover, not a gradient and not a full-bleed field. Cover and close are dark. Content slides are white.

## Grammar

One idea per page, in the product's own nouns. A headline can be a sentence the brand would actually say. Do not write a stack of adjectives. If the user supplied brand tokens, they override palette and type and they do not override the content rules below.

## Forbidden

Default purple gradients. Stock photographs of people you do not have rights to. Fake customer quotes. Fake ratings and unsourced claims about how many people use the product. Emoji. The cream-and-terracotta pair.

## Data

A price, a rating, or a reach number is included only when the prompt or a source file provides it. Otherwise leave it out. Illustrative layouts do not invent social proof.

## Chinese

Campaign lines in Chinese are short and specific. Do not translate an English slogan into 赋能 / 抓手 / 闭环.
